How it works

Four steps. Your financing stays the same.

1

Deposit

At closing, your down payment goes into a USDC vault on Stellar, tied to your loan agreement.

Try it → Testnet demo
2

Earn

The vault earns yield from vetted on-chain sources. Yield varies and can be zero.

Try it → Testnet demo
3

Sweep

Each month the yield goes to your lender as extra principal. You still make your payments; the yield offsets part of them.

Try it → Testnet demo
4

Payoff

Pay off the loan and the lender confirms the lien is released. Then the vault returns your deposit.

FAQ

Honest answers.

What if yield drops?

Then the sweep gets smaller, and it can be zero. Your loan payment doesn't change; you simply get less offset that month. There are no guaranteed returns.

What happens if I default?

The lender's normal process applies; they keep the lien and the title. Under the agreement, the lender can also claim the vault balance toward what's owed.

Does the yield pay off my loan?

No. You keep making your payments. The yield offsets part of them, either lowering your effective payment or ending the loan a little early.

Can I lose money?

Yes. On-chain yield sources carry smart-contract, market and stablecoin risk. Principal is at risk.

What happens to my deposit at payoff?

It's handled under the agreement terms. In the prototype, the vault balance can be applied to your final balance or released once the loan is paid off.

Is this live?

Not with real money. Quasaria Autopay is a prototype. A testnet demo runs on Stellar testnet with test tokens and simulated yield, ahead of a pilot with select dealers and lenders.
